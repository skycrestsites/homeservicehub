Is the Cleaning course really free?

Yes. The intro video and all 19 written guides are free to read right here in your browser. No card, no account and no signup. Premium adds a video lesson for every topic, 1-1 mentorship and hands-on help setting everything up.

What does Premium include?

A video lesson for every topic, 1-1 mentorship, help setting things up, a VA system setup, learning to run ads and get clients, and proper hiring with detailed guides and tricks. The details are on the course page.

Do I need the software to take the course?

No. The guides work with any tools. We recommend Crewty because it is what most owners in our community use, and guide 10 shows exactly how to set it up.

Who is in the Discord?

Owners at every stage, from people booking their first clean to operators running multiple crews. The help channels are open to everyone and answers are free.

What is the difference between the quick guide and the detailed guide?

The quick guide covers the essentials of each topic and is free for everyone. The detailed guide is the complete version of the same topic, with a full length video, templates and step by step walkthroughs. It is for members.

Is the quick guide really free?

Yes. All 19 Cleaning quick guides are free to read right here in your browser. No card, no account and no signup.

Do I need Crewty to take the course?

No. The guides work with any tools. We recommend Crewty because it is what most owners in our community use, and guide 10 shows exactly how to set it up.

Who is in the Discord?

Owners at every stage, from people booking their first clean to operators running multiple crews. The help channels are open to everyone and answers are free.

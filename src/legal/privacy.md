Home Service Hub ("we") runs learnhomeservices.com. This page explains what we collect and why. We keep it short because we collect very little.

## What we collect

**Waitlist emails.** When you join a waitlist we store your email address, the waitlist you joined and the time. We use it only to tell you when that course opens. We do not sell or share it.

**Accounts and Google sign-in.** If you create an account we store your name, email address, profile picture and how you sign in (Google or email and password) in Firebase Authentication. If you use Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We never see your Google password. Email passwords are stored by Firebase in hashed form. We use this only to sign you in, show your name on your account page and, when Premium opens, to give you access to what you bought. We do not sell it or share it with advertisers. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

**Course progress.** The free course saves which guides you completed in your own browser (local storage). It never leaves your device and we cannot see it.

**Analytics.** We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how people use the site, for example which pages are read and where people click. Clarity uses cookies and collects device and usage data. See the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

**Spam protection.** Waitlist forms may use Google reCAPTCHA to block bots. Its use is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Terms](https://policies.google.com/terms).

**Videos.** Course videos may be served by a video host such as Vimeo, Bunny or YouTube. They load only when you press play, and the host's own privacy policy applies.

## Where it is stored

Waitlist emails are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) in the United States. The site is hosted on Firebase Hosting.

## Your choices

You can ask us to delete your email at any time using the contact below. You can block analytics cookies in your browser settings and still use the whole site.

## Links to other sites

We link to tools and communities such as Discord, Facebook and Crewty. Their privacy policies apply once you leave this site.

## Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
